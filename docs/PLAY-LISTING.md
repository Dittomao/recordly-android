# TapSwoop — Google Play listing draft

**Title (25/30 characters):** TapSwoop: Screen Recorder

**Short description:** Record your screen, add tap-based auto-zoom, and edit on your device.

**Opening copy:** Record a walkthrough, tutorial, or app demo straight from your
phone. Guided setup gets tap tracking, the microphone and screen-sharing
readiness confirmed before your first take, and tap tracking creates smooth
zooms that follow what you touch. Adjust the zooms, trim the take, and save the
finished video to your gallery.

This is unpublished draft copy. Do not claim trademark clearance, Play
approval, system audio recording, or notification-shade capture. The Android
launcher label is **TapSwoop** and the production application ID is
`app.tapswoop.android`. Project formats are unchanged.
See [Google Play metadata requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842).
